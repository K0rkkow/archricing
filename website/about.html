ABOUT

ArchRicing is an independent Linux distribution project based on Arch Linux: a preconfigured system with KDE Plasma on Wayland, a graphical installer, a full visual identity and mouse-driven tools. Not a tiling rice. Not a dotfiles repo. A desktop with a personality.

[01] FLOATING ONLY

Windows stay where you put them.

[02] MOUSE FIRST

Click, drag, done. No cheat sheet.

[03] NO FORCED KEYS

Standard shortcuts keep working.

[04] RICE EVERYTHING

Sliders and switches, not config files.

LEGAL ArchRicing is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Arch Linux. Source-complete, no ISO released yet.
